# LDAC: design of record

**Bead:** `pico-link-cz0.5.3` · **Author:** Ada (architect) · **Date:** 2026-08-30
**Status:** design of record for M4 S4 (LDAC)

## Context

SBC over A2DP is merged (`55e6186`) and proven audible on real headphones. The
codec table (`firmware/src/codec_table.h`) was built in S1 specifically so LDAC
is "a new row plus an encoder, not surgery" (Andreas's ruling, quoted at
`codec_table.h:4-11`). This document decides what of that promise holds and what
does not.

## Decision

### Q1 — Where does libldac come from, and is the seam clean?

Sony/libldac, Apache-2.0, encoder-only. Vendored under `firmware/vendor/libldac`
with its own `CMakeLists.txt` producing a static lib, linked alongside
`pico_btstack_sbc_encoder` (`firmware/CMakeLists.txt:222-225`). Build **softfp**
to match pico-sdk's C for this board (`firmware/CMakeLists.txt:42-51`:
`thumb/v8-m.main+fp/softfp` — hardware single-precision FPU usable, VFP-register
argument ABI *not* used).

Two things must be measured, not assumed, in L0:

- whether libldac's default arithmetic path uses `double` (software-emulated on
  M33 → likely fatal) and whether a fixed-point build macro exists;
- `ldacBT_get_handle()` allocates on the heap. That is negotiation-time (thread
  context), not the IRQ hot path, so it is permissible — but the allocation must
  be confirmed to happen exactly once per stream and never from `encode()`,
  because `codec_table.h:87-92` states the encode contract as *no allocation, no
  logging, no blocking*.

USBPods is read-only. No byte of it may appear; the reviewer diffs against the
merge base per `.planning/design/2026-08-29-a2dp-source-pipeline.md:570-571`.

### Q2 — Does the codec table + BTstack actually support a vendor codec?

Yes, entirely, with no reshaping of the table:

- `pl_codec_t` already carries `vendor_id` / `vendor_codec_id`
  (`codec_table.h:60-62`); `avdtp_codec_type` accepts `AVDTP_CODEC_NON_A2DP`.
- `a2dp_source_create_stream_endpoint` takes arbitrary capability bytes and
  stores a pointer (`btstack/src/classic/a2dp_source.c:125-143`), so a static
  8-byte LDAC capability array works.
- LDAC's media codec information is 8 bytes, which is exactly
  `avdtp_stream_endpoint_t::media_codec_info[8]` (`btstack/src/classic/avdtp.h:634`).
  Exactly — no headroom. Worth a static assert.
- `a2dp_source_set_config_other()` exists (`a2dp_source.c:237-240` →
  `a2dp.c:1038-1059`). **Caveat:** it *stores the pointer* to
  `media_codec_information` (`a2dp.c:1051`), so that buffer must have static
  lifetime — a stack local here is a latent use-after-free.
- The negotiated config arrives as
  `A2DP_SUBEVENT_SIGNALING_MEDIA_CODEC_OTHER_CONFIGURATION` (forwarded at
  `a2dp.c:754-759`), raw bytes via `btstack_event.h:10233,10242`.
  `codec_table.h:75-82` already says each row owns its private config shape, so
  this needs no vtable change.
- `ENABLE_A2DP_EXPLICIT_CONFIG` switches off BTstack's SBC-hardcoded
  auto-selection (`a2dp.c:602-625`) and parks the state machine at
  `A2DP_DISCOVERY_DONE` after `A2DP_SUBEVENT_SIGNALING_CAPABILITIES_COMPLETE`
  (`a2dp.c:691-713`), waiting for us. **Our `a2dp.c` does not currently handle
  `CAPABILITIES_COMPLETE` at all** — that handler is new work, and it is where
  the preference-ordered table walk (S1 design section 4.3) lives.
- `btstack_config.h:73` `MAX_NR_AVDTP_STREAM_ENDPOINTS 1` must become 2;
  `btstack_config.h:101-105` already documents this switchover as S4's job.

### Q3 — CPU and pacing budget (the crux)

Two independent budgets; only one is currently known to fail.

**Pacing — fails today, deterministically.** Send rate is capped at one AVDTP
media packet per media-timer tick: the fill/arm happens once per tick
(`a2dp.c:673-685`) and one `CAN_SEND_MEDIA_PACKET_NOW` grant sends one packet
(`a2dp.c:1009-1011`). Real tick cadence is ~11.3 ms, not 10 (`a2dp.c:65-85`,
root-caused to `btstack_run_loop_async_context.c:51` and `:144-153`) →
**88.5 packets/s**. Required packet rates at a measured usable payload of ~883 B
(`frames_per_packet` = 7 with ~119 B SBC frames pins usable into [833, 951]):

| EQMID | bitrate @48 kHz | B/s | packets/s needed | vs 88.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HQ | 990 kbps | 123 750 | 140 | **fails, 1.58x** |
| SQ | 660 kbps | 82 500 | 93 | **fails, 1.06x** |
| MQ | 330 kbps | 41 250 | 47 | passes |

At our hard storage ceiling of 1029 B (`a2dp.c:105`, `:844`) HQ still needs
120/s. This is structural, not an MTU or buffer-size tuning problem.
`stop_packet_full_hot` (`a2dp.c:284-295`) was built as precisely this tripwire
and its own comment names the consequence: *"the deferred multi-packet queue
(bead pico-link-85v) becomes required work, not speculation."* LDAC above MQ
makes it required.

**CPU — unknown, bounded.** SBC measures 574 us/encode (`codec_sbc.c:128-132`)
x 375 frames/s ~= 21.5% of core0; per-tick dwell ~= 4.24 x 574 ~= 2434 us against
the 6000 us cap (`a2dp.c:126`). LDAC gets the same 375 calls/s (128 samples/frame
both sides — **verify `LDACBT_ENC_LSU` in the vendored header**), so the headroom
is ~= **2.4x, i.e. ~1400 us/frame**, before `stop_dwell` starts tripping at the
*average* rate — less once burst catch-up is allowed. Priority preemption
protects USB regardless (0xC0 worker over the 0xFF media IRQ,
`usb_pump.c:21-23`), so the failure mode of an over-budget LDAC is `stop_dwell`
and audible starvation, not a dead ISO endpoint. If it blows the budget, core1 is
the named escape hatch (S1 design section 5, lines 403-405) — *do not build it
pre-emptively*.

**On `pico-link-pbv`:** its fix is codec-agnostic by construction and carries
forward unchanged — the credit-pacing clock counts PCM sample-frames, not encoded
frames (`a2dp.c:216-231`). But **LDAC must not start on hardware until pbv is
closed and green**, because with an unsettled pacer you cannot distinguish "LDAC
is too slow" from "the pacer is broken", and that exact confusion already cost
pbv three design rounds.

### Q4 — Vtable change, and where the debt is

This is the one place the S1 seam does *not* hold. libldac owns its own
packetisation: `ldacBT_init_handle_encode()` takes the MTU, and `ldacBT_encode()`
returns `frame_num` and a complete payload only when full — it cannot be forced
to emit one frame per call (`LDACBT_MTU_REQUIRED` is ~679 B, well above LDAC's
~330 B HQ frame). Meanwhile `a2dp.c` owns packetisation today, accumulating
fixed-size frames into `sbc_storage` (`a2dp.c:452-514`). Two incompatible
contracts. `codec_table.h:31-34` already reserves `encoded_frame_bytes == 0` for
variable-length output, but **nothing implements it**: `a2dp.c:469` never trips
its packet-full test at 0, and `a2dp.c:536` yields `num_frames = 0`. Declared,
not built.

- **Sustainable:** make *every* row self-packetising. `encode()` gains an
  out-struct (`bytes_written`, `frames_emitted`, `payload_complete`); SBC's
  accumulator moves into `codec_sbc.c`, where it belongs. `a2dp.c` then has one
  uniform contract — "feed me 128 PCM frames, tell me when a payload is ready" —
  and gets *simpler*, with no codec-identity branch. **Cost:** rewrites the fill
  loop and the send path in the one code path currently proven audible.
- **Quick:** branch on `encoded_frame_bytes == 0`. ~20 lines. **Cost:** a
  codec-identity branch in all but name, in the exact file Andreas ruled must not
  have one (`codec_table.h:4-11`); AAC would inherit it, and the third row is
  where this kind of branch metastasises.

**Recommendation:** sustainable, sequenced *after* L1, so SBC is re-proven
audible against the new drain before the packetisation seam also moves.

### Q5 — Staging, regression risk, fallback

`ENABLE_A2DP_EXPLICIT_CONFIG` changes SBC's path too (`a2dp.c:602-625` compiles
out). The S1 design already names this: *"Stage 4 must re-prove Stage 1's 'SBC
audible' result, not just 'LDAC audible'"*
(`.planning/design/2026-08-29-a2dp-source-pipeline.md:263-265`). That belongs in
L2's acceptance, verbatim. SBC stays permanently last in `PL_CODECS` as the
mandatory fallback floor (`codec_table.h:97-99`); the table walk must fall
through to it on any LDAC `init()` refusal, and
`pl_bt_push_connect_succeeded(degraded=true)` (`a2dp.c:964-968`) becomes real for
the first time.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Shorten the media tick to 5 ms** instead of building the multi-packet
   drain. Gets ~159 packets/s, covering HQ — but doubles timer IRQ load and works
   only by leaning on pico-sdk's `+1 ms` truncation behaviour, which
   `a2dp.c:76-85` explicitly forbids depending on (*"NO DERIVATION IN THIS FILE
   MAY ASSUME 100 ticks/s"*). Rejected: cheap and wrong.
2. **Ship LDAC at MQ (330 kbps) only.** Fits today's drain with headroom. But
   LDAC at 330 kbps is not obviously better than SBC at ~357 kbps, so it delivers
   the marketing word without the product benefit. Acceptable as an *interim
   proof* that the row and negotiation work; not acceptable as the milestone.
3. **Give libldac a smaller MTU** so it emits more, smaller packets. Wrong
   direction — raises the packet rate, which is the thing already over budget,
   and is floored by `LDACBT_MTU_REQUIRED` anyway.
4. **Move encoding to core1 now.** Solves a CPU problem not yet measured and does
   nothing for the pacing problem that is actually blocking. Explicitly deferred
   by S1 design section 5.

## Consequences

- `pico-link-85v` graduates from speculative to required, and becomes the
  critical path for LDAC.
- `btstack_config.h` gains `ENABLE_A2DP_EXPLICIT_CONFIG` and
  `MAX_NR_AVDTP_STREAM_ENDPOINTS 2`; SBC's negotiation stops being BTstack's
  problem and becomes ours.
- The vtable's `encode()` signature changes, touching the proven SBC path — every
  stage after L1 must re-prove "SBC audible", not just "LDAC audible".
- A new third-party static lib enters the build with its own arithmetic-mode and
  heap behaviour to keep honest.

**Stopping rule, stated before the work starts:** if L0 measures > ~1400
us/frame at the target EQMID, STOP and re-design (core1) rather than pushing L3
through.

## Open decisions for Andreas

- **(a) Packetisation seam** — uniformise the vtable (right, but touches the
  proven-audible SBC path) vs special-case on `encoded_frame_bytes == 0` (cheap,
  and exactly the "no LDAC-or-SBC branch" rule set on 2026-08-29).
  Recommendation: uniformise, after L1.
- **(b) MQ-only interim** — accept MQ-only as a milestone, or gate LDAC entirely
  behind `pico-link-85v`. Recommendation: gate.
- **(c) LDAC logo/certification** is a Sony commercial licence question, separate
  from Apache-2.0. PoC-irrelevant, product-relevant.
